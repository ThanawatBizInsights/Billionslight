## Showcase episode (silent)

`src/episodes/phase3-engine-showcase.episode.json` (generated by
`scripts/review/build-phase3-showcase.mjs`) — 915 frames / 30.5 s, 5 scenes:

1. Cutaway, normal day, grid-tied (isometric → rooftop aerial).
2. Fade → grid outage with an on-grid inverter: anti-islanding shutdown, all
   appliances off despite strong sun; pole outage marker; living room; inverter.
3. Dip → hybrid + battery with configured backup output under clouds: outage at
   1.3 s, only backup-circuit loads (fridge, living-room light, fan) keep running;
   battery discharge (green).
4. Dip → living room: Ton walks to the switch and turns the lights on, Sunny
   does a head-scratch, walks over and waves; two-shot → tracking → close-up → medium.
5. Fade → garage: Sunny walks to the hybrid inverter and inspects it, Ton shades
   the eyes with a hand; grid outage switches to backup; tracking → equipment close-up → orbit.

Stills: `renders/phase-3-review/stills/` (plan: `stills-plan.json`).
Videos: `renders/phase-3-review/showcase/`.

## Known limitations / review points

- The cutaway is a wide house in a 9:16 frame: whole-house shots are small;
  story beats rely on cuts to the pole, rooms and equipment.
- Characters walk on straight keyframe lines; there is no path-finding around
  furniture (writers place keyframes).
- Consumption flows indicate "this circuit is powered", not measured kW per
  appliance. Simplified educational model — not a wiring diagram.
- Overlay text in the showcase only names the configuration; no technical
  claims are made, so no references are attached.
